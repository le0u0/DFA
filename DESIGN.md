# DFA visual direction

A desktop player enters an old arcade dungeon in a browser; the forest and combat should dominate, with a quiet dark frame around them.

Original DFO pixel-art scenery and sprites. Sharp nearest-neighbor scaling. Charcoal game shell, warm metallic gold primary actions, pale readable labels, red health and blue cooldown indicators. System sans for controls; restrained serif title treatment. Square-to-4px corners. No decorative card grids. Stable health/HUD positions; no page-load animation. Nonessential screen shake respects reduced motion.
