# Product

## Register

product

## Users

DFO players using a desktop keyboard to play an original-art browser adaptation.

## Product Purpose

Dungeon Fighter Arad (DFA). Immediate milestone: clear Mirkwood smoothly as Male Slayer or Male Gunner. Future scope: nine Grand Flores dungeons, with possible online co-op later.

## Brand Personality

Classic, adventurous, responsive. DFO PC is the visual and interaction reference.

## Anti-references

Generic dashboard layouts, placeholder forest illustrations, interchangeable class behavior, remapped PC action keys.

## Design Principles

- Combat takes the screen; menus stay short.
- Preserve original sourced character and environment artwork.
- Distinct melee and ranged combat.
- Readable health, telegraphs, cooldowns and exits.
- PC-default action keys, fixed for this version.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visible keyboard focus and semantic menu buttons. Text and shapes supplement combat colors. Clear keyboard instructions. Pause on focus loss; reduce nonessential effects when reduced motion is requested. No claim of full screen-reader accessibility for the real-time game.
